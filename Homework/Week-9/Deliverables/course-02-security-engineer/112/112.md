# OBJECTIVE
1. Create Policy

# Phase 1. Create Policy
The virtual machine is intended for an unmanaged instance group. therefore, an instance template is not needed in this configuration

1. navigate to cloud armor
1. Click create policy
1. enter name
1. click next step
1. click add target
1. backend target nginx-back
1. click next step
1. click create policy

## validation
1. click on the policy we created
1. pasted ip of load balancer in browser and refresh the page

Notes: takes a couple of minutes for the policy to take effect

# Phase 2. Add an second rule

1. Click add rule
1. enter a description
1. for match enter 0.0.0.0/0
1. action allow
1. priority 1,000
1. click add
1. action allow
1. click add

## validation
1. click on the policy we created
1. pasted ip of load balancer in browser and refresh the page

Notes: takes a couple of minutes for the rule to take effect
